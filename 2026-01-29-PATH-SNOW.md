## Reasoning
**Snowflake (SNOW)** is selected as the winning stock because of the extreme disconnect between today's price action (-7.6%) and its long-term fundamental position in the AI stack.

1.  **The Overreaction:** The market is selling off all software names today on fears that AI will replace SaaS. While valid for some, Snowflake provides the *data layer* required to train and run these AI models. The sell-off is indiscriminate.
2.  **Valuation:** At ~$200, SNOW is revisiting attractive support levels.
3.  **Analyst Backstop:** With 34 analysts carrying a "Strong Buy" and a target of ~$284, the institutional support remains intact despite short-term sentiment shifts.
4.  **Mean Reversion:** A stock of this quality dropping >7% on macro/sector news without a specific company-level disaster has a high probability of a technical bounce in the 5-10% range within days as value buyers step in.

**Prediction:** SNOW is likely to stabilize around $195-$200 and bounce back toward $215-$220 as the sector rotation cools off.
