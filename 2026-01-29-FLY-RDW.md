## Reasoning
**Redwire Corporation (RDW)** is selected as the winning stock because of the **asymmetric opportunity created by a liquidity event masking a fundamental breakout.**

1.  **The Catalyst:** The inclusion in the $151 billion SHIELD contract (Jan 27) is a game-changer for a company of Redwire's size (small cap), validating its technology for critical national defense.
2.  **The Setup:** The stock rightfully surged 30% on this news but was immediately hit by a large shareholder sale (2.8M shares). This non-fundamental selling pressure pushed the price down from ~$14.20 to ~$13.20.
3.  **The Trade:** Buying RDW now captures the fundamental value of the contract award at a discount caused by the temporary supply glut. Once the block sale is absorbed, the price should revert to reflecting the positive news.
4.  **Relative Value:** Compared to FLY (waiting for launch) or LUNR (already ran up), RDW offers a clear "mispricing" to exploit.

**Prediction:** RDW is likely to stabilize around $13.00-$13.20 as the block trade clears, then resume its upward trajectory toward $15-$16 as the market refocuses on the SHIELD contract revenue potential.
